Problem Statement & Ground Reality Analysis
Structural Gaps Across Rural & Tribal Maharashtra
🚗 The 40–80 km Travel Burden
Tribal families in Gadchiroli, Nandurbar, and Melghat travel hours on rough terrain for primary care, only to find clinics closed or doctors away, leading to fatal treatment delays.
📄 Fragmented Paper Health Records
Sub-Centres (ASHA) ➔ PHCs ➔ Sub-District ➔ Civil Hospitals operate in complete data silos. Over 78% of longitudinal patient history is lost across inter-facility transfers.
❌ 65% Referral Dropout Gap
When high-risk pregnant women or critical TB patients are referred upward, over 65% never reach the tertiary hospital due to lack of coordination, transit friction, and zero follow-up tracking.
📡 Zero-Connectivity & Stockout Blindness
Standard tele-health apps crash in 2G/offline remote valleys. Patients also travel long distances only to discover essential drugs (insulin, oxytocin) are out of stock.
Proposed Solution & Core Architecture
MahaHealth Connect: 4 Core Capabilities
📱 Frontline Assisted Triage
Empowers 65,000+ ASHA/ANM workers with an intuitive Marathi/Hindi tablet app to capture digital vitals (BP, SpO2, glucose) and automated risk scoring.
🎯 User: Village ASHA Workers
⚡ Offline-First Sync
Embedded SQLite + CRDTs ensure 100% operation without cellular internet. Syncs queued consults and records automatically when network is reached.
🎯 Core: Zero Data Loss
🔄 Closed-Loop Referral
Automated advance notification to District Hospitals before patient arrival, digital transit passes, and automated vernacular SMS/IVRS follow-up calls.
🎯 Impact: 0% Lost Patients
📦 Real-Time Drug Radar
Live inventory visibility across PHCs and Sub-Centres. Prevents blind referrals to clinics lacking stock and triggers automated drug re-orders.
🎯 Target: Zero Drug Stockouts

Implementation Feasibility & Rollout Plan
3-Phase Deployment Across Maharashtra
Phase 1: Tribal Pilot (M1–M3)
Target: Gadchiroli & Melghat Tribal Blocks

• 50 Sub-Centres, 10 PHCs, 1 District Hospital
• 200 ASHA workers trained on tablets
• Stress-testing offline CRDT sync and low-bandwidth WebRTC in remote valleys.
📍 200 Active Workers
Phase 2: District Scale (M4–M8)
Target: 5 High-Priority Rural Districts

• District Health Officer (DHO) live inventory radar
• Integration with 108 Emergency Ambulance API
• Vernacular IVRS automated follow-up calls in Marathi.
📍 5 Districts Scaled
Phase 3: State Scale (M9–M12+)
Target: 36 Districts of Maharashtra

• Full federation with India Health Stack (ABHA + e-Sanjeevani)
• Automated spatial outbreak prediction for communicable diseases
• White-label SaaS model for other state health missions.
📍 State-wide Deployment
Measurable Social Impact & Competitive Edge
Why MahaHealth Connect Wins in SIH 2026
60% Reduction
In unnecessary OPD travel through local village assisted tele-triage.
90%+ Completion
In high-risk referral compliance (reducing dropouts from 65% down to under 10%).
Zero Stockouts
Eliminates instances of patients traveling to clinics lacking essential medicines.
🏆 Key Unfair Advantages
⭐ Offline-Native: Unlike e-Sanjeevani which requires high-speed internet, MahaHealth is built from ground up for 0G/2G offline zones.
⭐ Assisted UX: Leverages trusted local ASHA workers rather than expecting illiterate tribal elders to navigate complex mobile apps.
⭐ Closed-Loop Tracking: Escalates automatically if a referred critical patient does not reach the destination hospital within 48 hours.
⭐ Integrated Medicine Radar: Real-time drug visibility linked directly with digital prescriptions.
